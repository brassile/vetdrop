Built for veterinary teams

VetDrop is designed to support practices with local last-mile medication delivery, without adding extra admin or changing how medications are prescribed or dispensed.

  • Same-day and scheduled delivery
  • London-only pilot service
  • Temperature-aware transport available by arrangement
  • Delivery confirmation available
  • No prescribing or dispensing by VetDrop

Important

VetDrop does not prescribe, dispense, alter or repackage medication. All medication must already be dispensed, labelled and sealed by the prescribing veterinary practice before collection.
